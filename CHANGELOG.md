# Aggie GPA 1.0

The first complete release of Aggie GPA, a native iPhone and iPad academic planning app.

## Highlights

- Course and assignment grade tracking with weighted categories, exams, score entry, and reminders.
- Separate Current, Projected, and Final grade outcomes; ungraded work is excluded rather than treated as zero.
- GPA planning, target-grade calculations, What-If simulation, and future-quarter planning without overwriting official records.
- Course insights and evidence-backed syllabus search with page-aware review and explicit confirmation before import.
- Optional on-device AI assistance with local model management and deterministic manual fallback.
- Quick Add with editable preview and confirmation before anything is saved.
- Native iPhone and iPad layouts with English and Simplified Chinese support.
- Local-first privacy, backups, privacy lock, and no account or cloud inference.
